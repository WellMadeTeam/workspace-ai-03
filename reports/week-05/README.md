# Week 05 report

## Project

Workspace for AI collaboration, team 3.

Our problem-space sentence: We solve the problem of teams who brainstorm with LLMs but lose context when sharing queries and results — they aim for seamless collaborative visual thinking where everything stays shared and evolves together.

## What we did

1. Planned [`SPRINT-05`](../../docs/project-plan.md#sprint-05) with a planned velocity of 26, kept the milestones [`MLS-05`](../../docs/project-plan.md#mls-05) and [`MLS-07`](../../docs/project-plan.md#mls-07) for the next increments, and added the stories `US-14`, `US-15`, and `US-16`.
2. Added `actionlint` and the product-code checks to the CI, with a test for each critical module of [`QR-02`](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-02.md).
3. Deployed the product to staging and production on the university VM, and wrote the [deployment runbook](../../docs/deployment.md).
4. Wrote a quality requirement test in every active High business-importance requirement, ran two of them, and recorded [the testing plan](../../docs/project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan).
5. Built highlighting, the text block's side panel, Mermaid diagrams in AI replies and on the board, clearing a block and deleting a message with a confirmation, and the reuse of a connected AI block's conversation as context, summarized when it is long.
6. Documented the features in [`docs/features.md`](../../docs/features.md), and carried out the Week 4 action points and open questions.
7. Showed the deployed product to the customer, reworked `US-05` after the meeting, redeployed, and released `REL-05` as [`v0.3.0`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/releases/tag/v0.3.0).

## Summary

- The product runs in two deployments on the university VM, staging and production, which the customer reaches from the university network; both run `debfe3e`, the commit tagged `v0.3.0`, as [`reports/week-05/deployment.md`](deployment.md) records.
- [`MLS-05`](../../docs/project-plan.md#mls-05), due in `SPRINT-05`, delivers the core flow: a teammate who joined by the link sends a query with answers from the board as its context, on a deployed product.
  [`MLS-07`](../../docs/project-plan.md#mls-07), due in `SPRINT-07`, delivers ten clients on one board with no lost update, shown by a repeatable test, and [`MLS-08`](../../docs/project-plan.md#mls-08) an outside team's brainstorming session.
- [`REL-05`](../../docs/project-plan.md#rel-05) is released as [`v0.3.0`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/releases/tag/v0.3.0) on 2026-10-31.
- Of the five product-code checks, the backend's linting, formatting, type checking, and build do not apply, because the repository configures no such tool for the Python backend; [`.github/workflows/product-code.yml`](../../.github/workflows/product-code.yml) records this beside its test step, and the frontend runs all five.
- `US-02`, `US-05`, `US-08`, and `US-14` were not accepted in this sprint and moved to `SPRINT-06`, so the actual velocity is 5 of the 26 planned points.
- The tests that are not `Passes` are in [the testing plan](../../docs/project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan).

## Findings

1. The customer accepted `US-06` with its acceptance criterion reworded, and `US-07`, per [`DEC-028`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-028) and [`DEC-030`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-030).
2. `US-05` was rejected because a connected AI block did not yet use another AI block's conversation as context, per [`DEC-029`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-029); the rework, which passes a summary of a long conversation, per [`DEC-032`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-032), was merged and deployed after the meeting, and the story stays open until the customer accepts it.
3. Diagrams render in the AI block beside their copyable source and reach the board only as images, per [`DEC-033`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-033) and [`DEC-034`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-034).
4. Highlighting lives in the right-click menu, and deleting asks for a confirmation, per [`DEC-035`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-035) and [`DEC-036`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-036); undoing a deletion stays out of scope, per [`DEC-037`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-037).
5. Still open: which brainstorming task the next validation session runs, carried in [the meeting report](meeting-report.md#previous-open-questions).
