# Sprint 05 retrospective

## What Went Well

| What went well                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The product was deployed by hand to staging and then to production on the university VM, and both run `v0.3.0`, tagged on `debfe3e`, as [the deployment report](deployment.md) and [the deployment runbook](../../docs/deployment.md) record.                                                                                                                  |

| The customer accepted `US-06` and the story for joining a board through a shared link at that meeting, per [`DEC-028`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-028) and [`DEC-030`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-030).                                                                                                                                                      |
| The critical modules gained automated tests in [#212](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/pull/212), and the backend pull requests of the sprint, such as [#225](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/pull/225) and [#227](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/pull/227), added router tests for their acceptance criteria. |

## What Did Not

| Problem                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Root cause                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The customer rejected `US-05` on 2026-10-29 because a connected AI block did not use another AI block's conversation as context, per [`DEC-029`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-029), so its rework ([#227](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/pull/227), [#229](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/pull/229)) merged only after the meeting and the story did not close. | Nobody checked the story's acceptance criteria against the deployed product before the meeting, so the team did not notice that AI conversations were left out of the context. |
| `US-06`'s acceptance criterion no longer described what the customer wanted and had to be reworded at the meeting before the customer accepted it, per [`DEC-028`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-028).                                                                                                                                                                                             | The story's description was not brought up to date with the customer's later decisions before it was presented.                                                                |
| Actual Velocity was `5` of the `26` planned points, `US-02`, `US-05`, `US-08` and `US-14` moved to `SPRINT-06`, and every feature pull request was opened on 2026-10-30 or 2026-10-31, with the release on the due date.                                                                                                                                                                         | The tasks for the stories were opened only after the meeting on 2026-10-29, and the actions on early pull requests and agreed endpoints from `SPRINT-04` were not carried out. |
