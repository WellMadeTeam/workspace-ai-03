## Project

Workspace for AI collaboration, team 3.

## Summary

The team is preparing `UT-01`; the recruitment target is five eligible participants, with at least three completed counted sessions. No usability results are claimed this week.

## Usability test

The participant profile and session plan are in [`docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md`](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#participant-profile), its [Schedule](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#schedule), and its [session-readiness check](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#session-readiness-check). The profile targets adults who work or study in software development or architecture, have taken part in team brainstorming about a software problem, and have used a conversational LLM for work or study in the past six months. The team will recruit outside its members, the customer, instructors, and course staff; five is the design target and at least three completed counted sessions are required.

Counted sessions are planned for SPRINT-08, in person on the university network; the team will arrange dates with eligible volunteers after the design review and when consent and data handling are ready. The teammate dry run does not count, and this report claims no usability result.

The blank [consent template](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/consent.md) is ready for use; completed forms and participant data remain private.
