# AI usage — Week 01

## Tools

Perplexity, Otter.ai

## What we used them for

Perplexity was used for building a wide candidate list.
Otter.ai was used for composing the transcript of the meeting recording.

## What we did with the output

Received search results were verified manually by several criterias:
- the link is openable and forwards to the existing product
- the product is relevant to our defined problem space

The received transcript was sanitized and converted to the format required by the assignment.

## What was not used

No generated text was submitted unchecked.
