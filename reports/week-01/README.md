# Week 01 report

## Project

Workspace for AI collaboration, team 3.

Our problem-space sentence: We solve the problem of teams who brainstorm with LLMs but lose context when sharing queries and results — they aim for seamless collaborative visual thinking where everything stays shared and evolves together.

## What we did

We researched three alternatives, compared them on six properties, and identified three gaps worth building on.

## Findings

The two strongest products solve routing well and data handling badly.
Nobody provides a decent AI collaboration tools, especially in terms of reusing the query and its results, which is the gap our project targets.
