# Kickoff meeting script

## Context

Our problem-space sentence: We solve the problem of teams who brainstorm with LLMs but lose context when sharing queries and results — they aim for seamless collaborative visual thinking where everything stays shared and evolves together.

## Questions

**Business goals**

1. _(open)_ Why did you decide to create own workspace instead of buying subscription to existing products?
2. _(open)_ When the reusing the queries and results during the brainstorming is possible, what is different about your work?

**End users**

3. _(open)_ Which specific types of artifacts did you use or your team used during the brainstorm?
4. _(open)_ Did you share the brainstorming session with external users (out of team, extranet), or you did it only privately (inside the team, intranet)? If you shared with extranet, in which format did you do it?

**Current workflow**

5. _(open)_ How you did the brainstorming with LLMs last time, step-by-step?
6. _(open)_ Have you demonstrated your AI sessions artifacts (query or/and results) to other members of your team? If yes, what you shared and how did you do it?

**Pain points and constraints**

7. _(open)_ What is the most annoying part of the brainstorming when you did it in existing solutions?
8. _(open)_ Did you reuse for new sessions the whole context of the project or specific sessions?

**Scope**

9. _(open)_ Is a hosted deployment acceptable to you, or it should have ability to be self-hosted?
10. _(open)_ Is the brainstorming space for visual thinking is acceptable to you, or it should also have the ability to store the notes and reuse them in upcoming brainstorms?

## Roles

Daniil asks, Anton takes notes, Danil and Kamil observe and record what we did not ask.

## Key improvements

**"Will be the tables, text blocks and drawings enough?" -> "Which specific types of artifacts did you use or your team used during the brainstorm?"**

We were offering a solution.
The rewrite asks for the actual needs in artifact types, so the answer describes a real necessity instead of our preference.

**"How do you reuse LLM queries and results as context?" -> "How you did the brainstorming with LLMs last time, step-by-step?"**

The original asks about abstract use case.
The rewrite anchors it to the brainstorming context, so the answer would provide the insights into the current workflow of the customer rather than we would have to made-up a link between abstraction and real usage.
