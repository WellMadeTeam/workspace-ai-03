# Week 04 report

## Project

Workspace for AI collaboration, team 3.

Our problem-space sentence: We solve the problem of teams who brainstorm with LLMs but lose context when sharing queries and results — they aim for seamless collaborative visual thinking where everything stays shared and evolves together.

## What we did

1. Planned [`SPRINT-04`](../../docs/project-plan.md#sprint-04) with its goal and a planned velocity of 26, moved the later sprints to the new Saturdays, and created the `MoSCoW`, `Story Points`, and `Priority` issue fields.
2. Wrote four quality requirements, with the mandated `Maintainability` / `Testability` requirement rated `High`, and their index as the priority matrix.
3. Wrote seventeen functional requirements behind the stories, each with its containers, its `shall` statements, and its own acceptance criteria.
4. Drew the container view of the four containers the product runs as, and linked the drivers into the architecture document.
5. Recorded three architectural decisions, [`ADR-001`](../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-001.md) to [`ADR-003`](../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-003.md), and their index, mapping each record to the requirements it addresses.
6. Built the "board is full" screen for the 11th member, the LLM error shown to every member, the deletion of blocks, the connections between blocks, the reuse of connected blocks as a query's context, and the list of the blocks an answer used, and refactored the frontend to Feature-Sliced Design and the backend into layers.
7. Carried out the Week 3 action points, and showed the sprint's stories to the customer against their acceptance criteria.
8. Brought the Week 2 and Week 3 issues to the Week 4 shape, wrote the glossary and the team's `AGENTS.md`, and closed the sprint with an actual velocity of 15 and its retrospective.
9. Cut the fourth release, [`REL-04`](../../docs/project-plan.md#rel-04), tagged [`v0.2.0`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/releases/tag/v0.2.0) on 2026-10-25.

[`US-05`: Reuse earlier answers as context](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/issues/41) and [`US-06`: See what context a query used](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/issues/42) moved out of `SPRINT-04` into [`SPRINT-05`](../../docs/project-plan.md#sprint-05), with 11 of the 26 planned story points: both are built and released in `v0.2.0`, but the customer has not judged them yet.
No candidate story moved out of the candidate this week.

## Findings

1. The customer accepted `US-01`, `US-04`, and the story for adding notes to the board, per [`DEC-022`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-022), [`DEC-023`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-023), and [`DEC-024`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-024).
2. Text blocks open the same side panel as AI blocks, so that a long text can be edited there and later used as context, per [`DEC-025`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-025).
3. Deleting a block needs protection against accidents, and the full-board message should say that the maximum of 10 members is reached rather than "10 out of 10".
4. The next sprint focuses on sharing diagrams and highlighting a part of the board, per [`DEC-026`](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-026).
5. Real-time sharing within two seconds for 10 members, [`QR-01`](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-01.md), is the quality attribute that shapes the architecture, and it is why board changes moved from polling to one WebSocket per member, per [`ADR-002`](../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-002.md).
6. Still open: which brainstorming task the next validation session runs, owned by @hrrrsss, and whether deletion is protected by an undo, a confirmation, or a harder-to-reach button, owned by @danmaninc and due in Week 5.
