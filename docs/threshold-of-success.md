# Threshold of success

## TOS-01

The customer accepts the core flow, where a team member puts a query and its response on the shared board and another member sends a new query with selected responses as its context, by the end of Week 5.

- **Status:** Open
- **Measure:** the customer's `DEC-nnn` accepting `US-01`, `US-04`, and `US-05`, which together make the core flow, at the Week 5 meeting, recorded in [decisions.md](decisions.md), by the end of Week 5.

## TOS-02

Per `CON-03`, 10 simultaneous clients edit one board for 30 minutes with no update lost and each change visible to the other clients within 2 s, by the end of Week 7.

- **Status:** Open
- **Measure:** a repeatable test, a script or a recorded session, of 10 clients on one board for 30 minutes showing 0 lost updates and every change visible to the other clients within 2 s, with its results linked from the Week 7 report, by the end of Week 7.

## TOS-03

A team of at least 3 people outside the development team completes one brainstorming session on the board, sending at least one query whose context is responses selected from the board, by the end of Week 8.

- **Status:** Open
- **Measure:** the session's board state, with the team's answer to "did you need a messenger?" recorded as a finding, in the Week 8 report, by the end of Week 8.
