# Assumptions

## ASM-01

Security management is important for teams.

- **Status:** Confirmed
- **How to check:** raise it at the Week 1 kickoff and verify it.
- **Outcome:** the customer accepted self-hosting with simple instructions, so teams can keep control of their data, per [`DEC-002`](decisions.md#dec-002), but told us not to focus on complex access controls, per [`DEC-004`](decisions.md#dec-004).

## ASM-02

Exporting vector and spatial data benefits the team's workflow, so they can further edit or enhance the brainstorming outcome.

- **Status:** Confirmed
- **How to check:** raise it at the Week 1 kickoff and verify it.
- **Outcome:** the customer called export a nice-to-have feature, so the team focuses on the core idea and picks one common format if time remains, per [`DEC-007`](decisions.md#dec-007).
