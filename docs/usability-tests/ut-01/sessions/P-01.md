## Observations and themes for review

These categories organize this participant's observations and paraphrased feedback; they do not establish recurring themes across independent sessions.
The original notes do not identify task boundaries or observation times, so the observations below are not assigned to tasks from inference.

### Editing and recovery

The participant did not realize that the side panel was in preview mode rather than edit mode. Dragging an existing connection did not reconnect it as expected. The Delete key did not work as expected, and the participant wanted Ctrl+Z to restore blocks.

### Reading and reusing replies

The observer noted verbose, unstructured replies, broken table formatting, and a reply area that was too small. The LLM service provider replied in English although the participant primarily used Russian. Copying text from AI blocks was inconvenient.

### Feature discovery

The participant had difficulty discovering diagram creation and proposed a dedicated toolbar button.
