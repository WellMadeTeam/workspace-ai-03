# UT-01: Participant Consent

This is the blank public template for the consent collected before every counted session.
Completed forms are private and must never be committed to this repository.

## What The Session Involves

You are invited to take part in a usability test of Workspace for AI Collaboration.
You will try planned brainstorming tasks on the staging product while a team member observes.
The team will observe the session and capture data and your activity during the session.
Before you sign, the facilitator will explain what will be captured and tell you who can access the captured data.
Capture will not start before you give consent.
You may stop the session at any time.

## How Your Information Is Used

The session-capture data, written consent, identity mapping, raw notes, working analysis, and any filtered test telemetry are private.
The team stores text records in its private usability-data repository.
It stores session-capture data in team-owned cloud storage.
The team members and course instructors can access those private materials for the course.
The team will publish only sanitized notes and aggregate results in the public project repository.
Your name, contact details, captured data, and other identifying information will not be published.

The team uses the staging product in a browser, human observers, telemetry collection tools, its private data repository, and team-owned cloud storage.
Before you sign, the facilitator will explain what will be captured, name the tools involved, and explain how copies they hold are deleted.
The team will not send your identity data to external generative AI. The team may share only the sanitized session note with external AI tools to analyze feedback about the product.
Copies held by the team or its tools, including the private repository and its history and the captured data, will be deleted after the course is graded.
The sanitized public record will remain.

## Your Choices

Participation is voluntary.
You may stop at any time by telling the facilitator during the session or using the private contact method the team gives you.
If you withdraw, the team will remove your raw notes and captured data and retire your participant code.
Your consent to session capture and to publication of sanitized public results is required for the session to count.
Your consent to an anonymized quotation is optional; declining a quotation does not affect whether the session counts.

Do not sign if you are under 18 or do not agree to both required items below.

## Consent

By signing, I confirm that:

- I am 18 years of age or older.
- I voluntarily agree to participate and to the session capture described to me before signing.
- I consent to the publication of sanitized notes and aggregate results that do not identify me.
- I understand that the team and course instructors can access the private materials, and that the private materials and tool-held copies will be deleted after the course is graded.
- I understand that I can stop the session or withdraw, and that my raw notes and captured data will then be removed and my participant code retired.

- [ ] I do not agree to publication of a quotation from my session.
