# QR-01: Show A Change To Every Member Within Two Seconds

With 10 members on one board, a change one member makes is visible to the other 9 within two seconds.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Performance Efficiency
- **Sub-characteristic:** Time Behaviour
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** High

## Scenario

### Source

A member working on a board that 9 other members have open.

### Stimulus

The member adds a block, moves a block, edits a block's text, or sends a query from an AI block; for a query, the change is the query message itself, not the LLM's reply.

### Environment

10 members, the most [`CON-03`](../../product-vision.md#con-03) allows, have the same board open in their browsers for 30 minutes, and the product runs on the customer's own computer, as [`DEC-002`](../../decisions.md#dec-002) allows.

### Artifact

The board as the `frontend` shows it, and the blocks and messages the `backend` keeps for it.

### Response

Every other member's board shows the change without a reload.

### Response Measure

Over a 30-minute session of 10 members on one board, every change is visible to the other 9 members within 2 seconds of the member making it.

## QRT-01

A change one of 10 members makes on a board on the staging deployment reaches each of the other 9 members within 2 seconds, over a 30-minute session.

- **Status:** Active
- **Level:** deployed
- **Runs at:** the staging deployment on the university VM, from a machine on the university network: `python scripts/check_sync_latency.py "$STAGING_URL"`.

### Procedure

1. Deploy the release candidate to the staging deployment on the university VM, and note its commit.
2. On a machine on the university network, from a checkout of the same commit, install the script's dependencies with `python -m pip install -r app/backend/requirements.txt`.
3. Set `STAGING_URL` to the staging deployment's address, and run `python scripts/check_sync_latency.py "$STAGING_URL"`.
   The script creates a board and connects 10 members to it, as the frontend does.
   For 30 minutes, the members take turns making a change every 5 seconds: they add a block, move it, edit its text, or send a query from an AI block.
   The other 9 members read the board as the frontend does: they poll the blocks every second and the AI block's conversation every 2 seconds.
4. Read the last lines of the script's output: the number of reads over 2 seconds and the number of changes a member never read, for each kind of change.

**Pass when:** the script reports 0 reads over 2 seconds and 0 missed reads across all four kinds of change.
