# QR-01: Show A Change To Every Member Within Two Seconds

With 10 members on one board, a change one member makes is visible to the other 9 within two seconds.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Performance Efficiency
- **Sub-characteristic:** Time Behaviour
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** High

## Scenario

### Source

A member working on a board that 9 other members have open.

### Stimulus

The member adds a block, moves a block, edits a block's text, or sends a query from an AI block; for a query, the change is the query message itself, not the LLM's reply.

### Environment

10 members, the most [`CON-03`](../../product-vision.md#con-03) allows, have the same board open in their browsers for 30 minutes, and the product runs on the customer's own computer, as [`DEC-002`](../../decisions.md#dec-002) allows.

### Artifact

The board as the `frontend` shows it, and the blocks and messages the `backend` keeps for it.

### Response

Every other member's board shows the change without a reload.

### Response Measure

Over a 30-minute session of 10 members on one board, every change is visible to the other 9 members within 2 seconds of the member making it.
