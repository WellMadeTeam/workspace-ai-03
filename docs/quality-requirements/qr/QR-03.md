# QR-03: Show A Failed Query's Error To Every Member

When the LLM service provider fails to answer a query, every member with the board open sees a plain-language error on that AI block within five seconds, and the board keeps working.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Reliability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Fault Tolerance
- **Business importance:** Medium
- **Technical risk:** Medium

## Scenario

### Source

The LLM service provider, which the `backend` calls for an AI block's reply.

### Stimulus

The provider returns an error, rejects the key, or gives no answer before the request times out.

### Environment

Several members have the board open, and the query was sent from one member's AI block, as [`DEC-018`](../../decisions.md#dec-018) requires the error to reach all of them.

### Artifact

The AI block's conversation, as the `backend` stores it and the `frontend` shows it.

### Response

Every member sees on that AI block an error that says, without technical detail, that the query got no answer, and every member can still edit the board and send another query.

### Response Measure

In 10 of 10 forced provider failures, every member with the board open sees the error on that AI block within 5 seconds of the failure, and an edit made right after it reaches the other members as [`QR-01`](QR-01.md) requires.
