# FR-010: Delete A Block

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-03`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/issues/39), [`US-15`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/issues/202), [`DEC-036`](../../decisions.md#dec-036)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`
- **Changed:**
  - Deleting a block asks the member for confirmation first, `AC-01` was edited in place, and `AC-02` was added for a cancelled deletion, per [`DEC-036`](../../decisions.md#dec-036); undoing a confirmed deletion stays out of scope, per [`DEC-037`](../../decisions.md#dec-037).

## Behavior

When a member chooses to delete a block, the product shall ask that member to confirm the deletion.

When the member confirms the deletion, the product shall remove the block from the board of every member.

When the member cancels the deletion, the product shall keep the block, its content, and its connections on the board of every member.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a block on the board, when a member chooses to delete it and confirms, then no member sees it, also after a reload.

### AC-02

Given a block on the board, when a member chooses to delete it and cancels the confirmation, then every member still sees the block with its content and its connections, also after a reload.
