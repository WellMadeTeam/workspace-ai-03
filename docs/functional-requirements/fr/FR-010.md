# FR-010: Delete A Block

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-03`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/issues/39)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When a member deletes a block, the product shall remove it from the board of every member.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a block on the board, when a member deletes it, then no member sees it, also after a reload.
