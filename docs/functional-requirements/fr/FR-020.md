# FR-020: Put A Rendered Diagram On The Board As An Image

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-08`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/issues/44), [`DEC-034`](../../decisions.md#dec-034)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When a member puts a diagram rendered in an AI block on the board, the product shall add the rendered picture to the board as an image that every member sees.

When a diagram cannot be rendered, the product shall not add it to the board and shall tell the member why.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a diagram rendered in an AI block, when a member puts it on the board, then every member sees the same picture on the board as an image, also after a reload.

### AC-02

Given diagram code that could not be rendered, when a member tries to put it on the board, then no image is added and the member sees that the diagram could not be rendered.
