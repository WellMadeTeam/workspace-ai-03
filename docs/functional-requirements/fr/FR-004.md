- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-01`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/issues/37), [`DEC-003`](../../decisions.md#dec-003), [`DEC-017`](../../decisions.md#dec-017), [`CON-03`](../../product-vision.md#con-03), [`ADR-002`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-002.md), [the Week 5 meeting report](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/blob/main/reports/week-05/meeting-report.md#action-points)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`
- **Changed:**
  - The message the 11th member sees says that the board has reached its maximum of 10 members instead of only that the board is full, and `AC-01` was edited in place, per the action point "Reword the "board is full" message to say that the maximum of 10 members is reached" of [the Week 5 meeting report](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/blob/main/reports/week-05/meeting-report.md#action-points).

## Behavior

When 10 members already have a board open and an 11th opens it, the product shall not connect the 11th member and shall show a message that the board has reached its maximum of 10 members.

When one of the 10 members leaves the board, the product shall let the next member who opens it connect.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given 10 members on a board, when an 11th opens it, then the 11th sees a message that the board has reached its maximum of 10 members and none of the board's blocks, and the 10 members keep working on the board.

### AC-02

Given an 11th member was turned away, when one of the 10 leaves and the 11th opens the board again, then the 11th member sees the board.
