# FR-011: Highlight A Block

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-02`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/issues/38), [`DEC-006`](../../decisions.md#dec-006), [`DEC-035`](../../decisions.md#dec-035)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`
- **Changed:**
  - A member highlights a block from the block's right-click menu, and the toolbar offers no highlight, with the statement and `AC-03` added, per [`DEC-035`](../../decisions.md#dec-035).

## Behavior

The product shall let a member highlight a block from that block's right-click menu, and shall offer no highlight on the toolbar.

When a member highlights a block, the product shall show it highlighted to every member until a member removes the highlight.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a block, when a member highlights it, then every member sees it highlighted.

### AC-02

Given a highlighted block, when a member removes the highlight, then no member sees it highlighted.

### AC-03

Given a block, when a member opens its right-click menu, then the menu offers to highlight the block, and the toolbar shows no highlight control.
