# FR-008: Show The Blocks A Query Used

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-06`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/issues/42), [`DEC-014`](../../decisions.md#dec-014)
- **Containers:** `frontend`

## Behavior

When a member selects an AI block, the product shall show how many blocks are connected to it as its context, and which ones.

When a member picks one of the listed blocks, the product shall bring that block into view.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given an AI block connected to three blocks, when a member selects it, then the member sees three entries, one per connected block.

### AC-02

Given an AI block connected to nothing, when a member selects it, then the member sees that it has no connected blocks.
