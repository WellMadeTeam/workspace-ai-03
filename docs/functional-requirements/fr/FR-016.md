# FR-016: Sign In To An Account

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-11`](https://github.com/WellMadeTeam/workspace-ai-03/issues/47)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When someone signs in with an account's name and password, the product shall sign them in and show that account's boards.

When someone signs in with a wrong password, the product shall not sign them in and shall show an error.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given an account that created a board, when its owner signs out and signs in again with the right password, then they see that board in their list.

### AC-02

Given an existing account, when someone signs in with a wrong password, then they stay signed out and see an error.
